Delete your Revora account

This page describes how to permanently delete your Revora account and the data associated with it.

Delete from inside the app (immediate)

  1. Open Revora and sign in
  2. Go to More → Settings
  3. Tap Delete account
  4. Confirm with your password

Deletion is permanent and takes effect immediately.

Or request deletion by email

Email skriaz30@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my Revora account”. We complete emailed requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

Everything: your account, every motorcycle, fuel log, odometer reading, maintenance rule, service record, part, accessory, expense, document, warranty, condition check and photo. Nothing is retained after deletion and there is no recovery.

Deleting data without closing your account

You can delete any individual record — a fuel log, a service, a document — from inside the app at any time, and it is removed from your account immediately.

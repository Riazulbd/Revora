Revora · Know your bike

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

What Revora stores

The motorcycles you add and everything you log against them: fuel fill-ups, odometer readings, maintenance rules and completions, services, parts, accessories, expenses, documents, warranties and condition checks. Your display name, your email address if you signed up with one, and your unit and currency preferences. Photos you choose to attach.

Where it is stored

On your device, and in your own private area of Revora's Firebase (Google Cloud) project so the same records appear wherever you sign in. All transfer is encrypted in transit (TLS). Access is enforced on the server: only your signed-in account can read or write your records.

Advertising

The free plan shows one banner advert, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to serve and measure ads; see Google's advertising policies. Your motorcycle records are never shared with advertisers. The paid plan shows no adverts.

What Revora does not do

Revora does not sell your data and does not use your records to train anything. The in-app assistant runs on your device and answers only from figures the app has already calculated. Revora does not request your location or contacts.

Permissions

Notifications are requested only when you turn reminders on. The camera is requested only when you choose to take a photo. Choosing an existing photo uses the system picker and needs no permission.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account permanently removes your account and every record in it. You can also delete any individual record at any time. See how to delete your account.

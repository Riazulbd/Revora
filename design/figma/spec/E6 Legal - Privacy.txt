[Section · The Pro assistant] display: flex; flex-direction: column; align-items: flex-start; padding: 0px 4px; gap: 6px; width: 358px; height: 290px; align-self: stretch
[The Pro assistant] font-weight: 700; font-size: 16px; color: #14171B; align-self: stretch
[On Revora Pro, a question you type into the assistant is sent to Revora’s server together with a summary of that motorcycle’s figures — its odometer, mileage, running costs, service status and recent fill-ups — and passed to a language model (Google Gemini, via OpenRouter) to phrase the answer. Only the summary is sent, never your documents, photos, registration or chassis numbers, and never your name or email. Your question and the answer are not stored: only the date, the model used and the number of tokens, so usage can be reconciled.] font-weight: 400; font-size: 14px; color: #3B4048; align-self: stretch

Your garage How mileage is calculated
335 km÷9.0 L=37.2 km/L
Revora measures the distance between two full-tank fills and divides it by the fuel used in between. Partial fills roll into the next full-tank interval; adjustments (spillage, leaks, service use) are excluded so the number stays honest. One reliable interval so far — averages and best/worst unlock at five. Export fuel history
CSV and Excel-style exports are a Pro feature.

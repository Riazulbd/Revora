In short — a summary, not the contract
Revora calculates from what you log. Billing lives in Google Play. It is a logbook, not an inspection, and the Proof Pack certifies its own provenance — not the motorcycle.

Your figures
Every figure Revora shows is calculated from what you logged. Consumption is measured between two full-tank fills; where there is not enough data, Revora says so rather than estimating. Incorrect entries produce incorrect figures — you can edit or delete any record and everything derived from it is recalculated.
Subscriptions
Pro is billed by Google Play. Renewal, cancellation and refunds happen in Play, not in the app. If Pro lapses, your records stay — screens that need Pro say so instead of showing them.
What Revora does not warrant
Revora is a logbook. It is not a mechanical inspection, not a diagnosis, and not advice about whether a motorcycle is safe to ride. Maintenance reminders repeat what you told them; they do not replace a mechanic’s judgement.
Limits of the Proof Pack
The stamp certifies that Revora generated the document from the owner’s records at that moment — record count, dates covered, and a reference that fingerprints exactly those records. It says nothing about the motorcycle itself.
